SmartBear integrated vision AI into TestComplete to address persistent automation challenges that enterprise testing teams face, including brittle tests that break with UI changes and applications that lack accessible properties. The solution helps teams reduce time spent on test maintenance rather than test creation, enabling more scalable and resilient UI test automation across complex applications.
